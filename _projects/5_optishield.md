One-time-password scams trick people into reading verification codes to attackers. OptiShield AI sits between the user and incoming messages and flags OTP requests that look like social-engineering attempts before the code is shared.

Built in 60 hours with Rashmi Elavazhagan and Shreyas Chaudhary as Team Trio at Cal Poly Pomona's **AI for a Better Future** Fair and Hackathon in May 2025, where 239 students on 51 teams competed across seven challenges. The project took **third place in the Future of Business track**.
